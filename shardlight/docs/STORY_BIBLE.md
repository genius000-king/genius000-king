# SHARDLIGHT — Story bible

> Spoilers. This file is the single source of truth for plot, characters, reveals and tone.

## Logline
A girl wakes in the crater of the lab that shattered her town into seven worlds — and every piece she
recovers proves that the world broke **to make room for her**.

## Tone
Warm, luminous, curious on the surface (Slime Rancher, Outer Wilds, Ghibli); grief and moral weight
underneath. Characters are kind and funny; the mystery is melancholy. Never grimdark; never cynical.
Revelations are delivered through *environmental discovery* (echoes, objects, places) more than exposition.

## The world before (the truth, hidden from the player)
- **Harrow's Reach** (حارة الميناء): a coastal town under a hill. On the hill: the **Meridian Institute of
  Resonance**, which studies "adjacent possibility" — the faint vibration other outcomes leave in matter.
- **Dr. Idris Vael** (د. إدريس فايل), lead physicist. Widower. His daughter **Rae** died at 16 in a
  boating accident during a storm (spring, one year before the game). He blamed himself: he was at the
  Institute that night instead of picking her up.
- Idris built **the Loom** (النَّوْل): a ring that "weaves" a thread between our world and a nearby
  possibility. Official purpose: measurement. Secret purpose: find a possibility where Rae survived.
- **04:17, the night of the Fracture**: Idris found a thread where Rae lived. He did not look through it —
  he *pulled*. Rae from that possibility came through. Reality could not hold two incompatible histories in
  one place; it split along its seven fundamental "laws" into seven shards, each keeping one law bent.
  The Loom's prism core burst into seven **Keystones**, one embedded in each shard, each pinning that
  world's broken law in place.
- Idris was at the core. His body was lost; his mind was imprinted into the Institute's prototype safety
  drone, which wakes with fragmented memory and the name tag **LUMEN**.

## Endings (chosen at the Loom with all seven Keystones)
1. **Restore** (استعادة): Re-weave the original world. The shards fold back; Rae fades, and Idris stays
   lost at the core — the restored world simply never had the Fracture's survivors. Final image: Harrow's
   Reach at dawn, a kite in the sky, Wren's hive, nobody remembers. Bittersweet.
2. **Weave** (نَسْج): Bind the seven Keystones into a new lattice: one strange world where all laws stay
   broken and everyone lives — including Rae. The sky scar heals into an aurora. Joy with a cost: the world is
   permanently unstable (post-game "Patchwork" free roam).
3. **Return the Thread** (secret — requires all 49 Memory Echoes): Rae uses Rewind + Prism Sight to reach
   04:16 and speaks to Idris *through* the Loom before he pulls. She convinces him to let go — and asks him to
   send her back gently instead. Her original world (where her mother died and her own Idris waits) is
   restored; this world is mended; Idris finally grieves. The last line is Lumen/Idris: "Go home, little
   light." Post-credit: in Rae's home world, a drone prototype on her father's desk flickers on.

## Mystery mechanics
- **Memory Echoes** (صدى الذاكرة): 7 per world (49 total). Floating prism fragments; touching one plays a short
  painterly memory scene (audio + text) and adds a journal entry. They form the "real" story in fragments.
- **Lumen's gaps**: Lumen's dialogue contains glitches `[—DATA LOST—]` exactly where it hides things. The
  journal later restores those lines in a different color after reveal 5.
- **Versions**: Each townsperson's journal page grows a "versions" list as Rae meets them across shards.
- **The 04:17 clock**: every world has a stopped clock showing 04:17 hidden somewhere (collectible).

## Act structure for the vertical slice (M1)
1. **Prologue — "04:17"**: black screen, a heartbeat, a voice ("Rae? Rae, can you hear me?" — Lumen). Wake in
   the crater. Tutorial: look, walk, jump, interact with Lumen. Pick up the **Resonator** gauntlet from a
   shattered case. Lumen explains: seven shards, seven Keystones, "if we return them to the Loom, perhaps we
   can put everything back." First gate (Verdant) flickers awake.
2. **Verdant Drift**: Arrive at the gate. Meet **Pip & Tansy** (kites, low-gravity jumps tutorial). Village
   of **Old Marrow** & **Ilse** (gardeners) → quest *"The Bloom Bell"*. **Wren** at the floating hives.
   Three trials:
   - **Trial of Drift** (trial_float): platforming across floating islands in low gravity, reach a bell.
   - **Trial of Bloom** (trial_bloom): defend the Great Bloom from **Static** (glitch creatures) with the
     Resonator → combat tutorial (Plasma Lance charge shot + Shard Scatter).
   - **Trial of Echo** (trial_echo): find 3 memory echoes in the hollow log grove (environmental puzzle).
   Each trial lights a petal of the **Sanctum** seal. Sanctum: a mountain-top shrine; the Keystone floats in
   a storm of petals; a **Static Warden** guards it (mini-boss). Taking the Keystone: gravity "snaps" — Lift
   ability unlocked with a big VFX/audio moment.
   Reveal R1 delivered by Marrow on return: "the Vael girl who drowned last spring." Rae stops. Lumen: silent
   for a beat. "...[—DATA LOST—]."
3. **Return to the Loom**: install Keystone #1. The Loom projects a vision (the stormy harbour, a small boat,
   a lantern). Ending sting. Amber gate wakes. (Slice end card: "To be continued — 1/7 Keystones".)

## Writing rules
- Bilingual: every line has `ar` (Modern Standard Arabic, natural and warm; light colloquial flavour allowed
  for kids and Marrow) and `en`.
- Lines ≤ 160 characters. Personality in word choice, not in exposition dumps.
- Lumen speaks precisely, sometimes with numbers; Rae is wry; kids are breathless; Marrow rambles kindly;
  Wren speaks softly, in images.
- Every NPC gets: a greeting, 2–4 topic branches, a goodbye, and a post-quest line. Optional banter lines
  for ambient barks.
