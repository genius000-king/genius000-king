# SHARDLIGHT — Product brief

> **ضوء الشظايا** · Open-world, first-person, single-player mystery adventure · Godot 4.7 · Windows + Android

## Player fantasy
You are **Rae Vael**, 17, who wakes in the crater of the Meridian Institute after *the Fracture* — the night a
machine called the **Loom** split the coastal town of Harrow's Reach into seven floating worlds, each obeying a
different law of physics. With a tuning-fork gauntlet (the **Resonator**) and a small prism drone (**Lumen**), you
travel the shards, recover the seven **Keystones** that hold each world's broken law, and slowly learn that the
accident was not an accident — and that *you* are the reason the world broke.

## Pillars
1. **Light is the main character.** Painterly stylized 3D (Slime Rancher warmth, reference sheet of Rae), saturated
   but harmonized palettes per world, glow, auras, energy, volumetric light. Every ability *reads* as light.
2. **Each world is a different law.** Low gravity, uneven time, local gravity, sound-as-force, solid light, reverse
   growth. A world's law shapes traversal, puzzles, combat, and its look.
3. **Every discovery reframes the story.** Lore is found, not told. Seven reveals, each recontextualising the last.
4. **Tactile feel.** Footsteps per surface, run cadence, plasma that *gathers* before it fires, recoil, smoke,
   heat-haze, camera response.

## Full vision (all user requirements kept)
- Very large open world: Hub crater + 7 shard worlds, each a large explorable landscape with distinct
  biome, flora, architecture, caves, NPCs, creatures.
- Hundreds of unique models (target 300+ unique meshes across packs, before procedural variation).
- Stories, dialogue, side characters, branching conversations, journal, memory echoes, shocking reveals, 3 endings.
- Weapons and abilities with heavy VFX + animation + audio (plasma gather → release, shards, pulse, lift, stasis…).
- First-person body awareness: arms/gauntlet viewmodel, shadow, footsteps.
- Platforms: Windows (Forward+ renderer) and Android (Mobile renderer), touch controls.

## Honest scope statement
A literal AAA game that out-details Genshin Impact is the product of hundreds of people over years. This project
does not pretend otherwise. What this studio run delivers is **a real, playable, exportable vertical slice** of the
full game, built on systems designed to scale to the full vision, with the art and story bible to continue.
Claims in DELIVERY.md are limited to what was demonstrated and inspected.
